Requirements

Eligibility Criteria for First Year

  1. S.S.C. (10th) Pass with a minimum of 35% marks.
  2. Candidates are advised to follow the rules for admission to the First Year Diploma in Engineering course as published by the Directorate of Technical Education from time to time.
Documentation

Documents Required for First Year

  1. Allotment Letter (printout from internet)
  2. Std X Mark Sheet (SSC)
  3. Std X Passing Certificate (SSC)
  4. Leaving Certificate
  5. Nationality Certificate
  6. Caste Certificate (all reserved category candidates)
  7. Caste Validity Certificate (all reserved category candidates)
  8. Non-Creamy Layer Certificate
  9. Migration Certificate (students passing Std X from a board other than Maharashtra)
  10. Gap Certificate (in the form of an affidavit on ₹100/- non-judicial stamp paper)
  11. Latest colour photographs — total 5 (3 passport size & 2 stamp size)
Note: All above listed original documents must be submitted along with two attested photocopies of each document.
Financial Support

Scholarship

Scholarships are available for all SC, ST, NT, VJ, OBC, SBC, and Minority (Jain, Buddhist, Christian, Sikh, Parsi, Muslim) students, as per applicable Government rules.
