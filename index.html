Meet Our Founder

LOKNETE MADHUKARRAO PICHAD

Founder, Akole Taluka Education Society
"True progress for our tribal and rural communities lies in the twin pillars of education and self-reliance."

Loknete Madhukarrao Pichad (1941–2024) was a distinguished statesman who represented Akole in the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly for seven terms and served as Cabinet Minister for Tribal Development. A lifelong champion of rural upliftment, he founded the Akole Taluka Education Society to bring quality technical education to the youth of this region.

Loknete Madhukarrao (Kashinath) Pichad

1 June 1941 – 6 December 2024
Former Cabinet Minister, Government of Maharashtra

Madhukar Kashinath Pichad was an Indian politician, social worker, and Cabinet Minister in the Government of Maharashtra. He represented the Akole Assembly constituency in the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly for seven consecutive terms from 1980 to 2014, and served as Leader of the Opposition in the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly from March 1995 to July 1999.

Born on 1 June 1941 into a tribal family of the Mahadeo Koli community in Rajur, Ahmednagar district, he completed his B.A. LLB from Ferguson College, Pune, where he first became involved in student politics. He began his public career as a Zila Parishad Member and Chairman of Panchayat Samiti Akole Taluka in 1972.

A member of the Nationalist Congress Party for most of his career and later its Maharashtra President, he joined the Bharatiya Janata Party in 2019 along with his son, Vaibhav Pichad. Beyond politics, he founded the Amrutsagar Milk Co-operative in Akole in 1961 and served as founding president of the Agasti Sahakari Sugar Factory — India's first cooperative sugar factory, established in 1993.

Ministerial Positions Held

  • Cabinet Minister for Tribal Development (1991–1992), Sudhakarrao Naik ministry
  • Cabinet Minister for Tribal Development, Dairy Development, Travel Development & Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (1993–1995), Fourth Pawar ministry
  • Cabinet Minister for Tribal Development, third term (1999–2003), First Deshmukh ministry
  • Cabinet Minister for Tribal Development, Nomadic Tribes and Other Backward Class Welfare (2013–2014), Prithviraj Chavan ministry

Carrying the Legacy Forward

VAIBHAV PICHAD

President, Akole Taluka Education Society
"Our responsibility is to build on this foundation — bringing new opportunities to the youth of Akole while staying rooted in the values my father established."

Vaibhav Madhukar Pichad continues his family's legacy of public service, having represented Akole in the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly and currently serving as National Minister of the BJP Scheduled Tribes Morcha. A key figure in the region's cooperative movement, he chairs the Amrutsagar Milk Co-Operative Union and remains deeply committed to advancing education and opportunity for the youth of Akole taluka.

About the Institute

Four decades of diploma engineering education in Akole.

Established in 1983, the institute has grown into one of the region's trusted centres for diploma engineering, offering programmes in Civil, Computer, Electronics & Telecommunication, and Mechanical Engineering, supported by a strong Applied Science foundation year.

Approved by AICTE and affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), the institute follows a curriculum that balances classroom instruction with hands-on workshop training, industrial visits, and structured placement preparation.
