Who We Are

About the Institute

Engineering diploma education has always been an important driving force behind the technological innovations for a country's development, growth, and prosperity. Faculty of Polytechnic, Akole is a private, self-financed institution established by the Akole Taluka Education Society (ATES), Dist. Ahmednagar, Maharashtra state, in the year 2014.

The trust ATES was established in 1971–72 under the dynamic and visionary leadership of Late Mr. Dadasaheb Rupwate and Honourable Mr. Madhukarrao Pichad (Ex-Minister of State, Tribal Development, Maharashtra State), with an objective of providing quality education in the fields of Art, Commerce, Engineering, Computer Application, Business, and primary education from kindergarten onwards. FOP, Akole is located 1.5 kms away from Akole bus stand.

FOP, Akole works with a vision to achieve excellence in education, research, and service to industry, and to develop in students a sense of moral duty and responsibility toward society through various co-curricular and social activities.

A Note of Heritage

The Legacy of Lord Agasti

We would like to make a humble note of our richest cultural heritage — that of Lord Agasti. He is remembered as the first Arya to cross the Vindhya mountain ranges and settle at this very place, where a famous temple of Lord Agasti stands today on the banks of the river Pravara. Agasti was the pioneer grammarian of the Tamil language and a social revolutionary whose marriage to Lopamudra is regarded as the first inter-caste marriage. It is in keeping with this tradition that we welcome you all to our campus.
