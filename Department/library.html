Overview

Library

ATES Faculty of Polytechnic Library is "student-centric," user-focused, innovative, and excellence-driven. The Institute Library manages knowledge in both print and digital formats, and provides faculty, students, and staff with professional support to find, evaluate, manage, and use these resources. It provides a high-quality ambiance for both reflective and collaborative work and study. Its collection of about 6,000 items is used by more than 1,026 members and others. The library extensively uses social media for improved communication and interaction — a reflection of its continuous engagement with users as a valuable partner in their learning and research.

ATES Faculty of Polytechnic Library plays an important role in acquiring, organizing, and disseminating information. It is situated on the second floor, with a carpet area of 400 sqm. The library has a rich collection of textbooks, reference books, and national journals, along with bound volumes in the fields of General Science and Diploma Engineering. Books are classified as per the Dewey Decimal Classification system (DDC 22nd Ed.). The library provides automated services using 'E-Granthalaya & Latest KOHA' software application, with all books bar-coded for fast circulation and searching.
